Questions creators ask

改：按 API 方案第六节补齐达人最常问的五问
What if I want to stop?

Open this page and tap Disconnect, or revoke BLOOM GLOW in your TikTok Shop authorization settings. After that we can't do anything on your account.

Can you move my money?

No. The authorization has no transfer or withdrawal permission. TikTok settles commission straight to your account.

Can I delete a video you posted?

Yes. Delete it in TikTok like any video of your own.

Will this affect my own posts?

No. Keep posting as usual. TikTok limits shoppable videos per day (3 a day under 5,000 followers, 30 a day after), and we stay inside that limit.

How long does the authorization last?

Until you disconnect or revoke it, or TikTok's authorization expires. We remind you before it expires.

Is this free?

Yes. You never pay us anything. We earn only our share of the commission when a product sells.

Do you need my password or login?

No. You authorize on TikTok Shop's own page. The permission list doesn't include your password, your messages or your buyers' addresses.

Do I need to make or post videos?

No. After you authorize access, we handle product selection, video creation, publishing and promotion.

Are the videos AI-made?

Partly. We use AI tools and real product footage, and every video carries TikTok's AI-content label where required.

How do I get paid?

By TikTok Shop, like any affiliate sale. Your commission is settled straight to your own TikTok Shop balance.
